    You take the quiz

    Two options at a time. No account, no email, nothing to sign up for.

  2. 02

    You get a link

    Your answers travel inside the link itself. Send it by message, or post it.

    They take it too

    They answer the same quiz. At the end, both results sit side by side with a match score.
